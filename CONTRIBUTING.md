# Contributing to WatchSync

Thanks for wanting to help! Fixes and improvements are welcome as pull requests: they are
reviewed, and merged once they are tested.

## Before you start

- For anything bigger than a small fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.
- WatchSync stays free of ads, trackers, analytics and accounts. Pull requests adding any of
  these won't be merged.

## Pull requests

- One topic per pull request, with a short description of what changes and why.
- Say how you tested it: which phone and watch, which Android and Wear OS versions.
- Match the style of the surrounding code; keep user-visible text in `strings.xml`.
- If AI tools helped you, that's fine (WatchSync itself is built that way): just say so in the
  description, and make sure you understand and have tested what you submit.

[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) — add a `Signed-off-by:`
line to your commits (`git commit -s`).

## Reporting bugs

Open an issue with: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and your phone and watch
models with their Android / Wear OS versions.
