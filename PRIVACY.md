# WatchSync privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

WatchSync is a free, open source app by SanniniciStyle that keeps an Android phone and a Wear OS
watch in step (Do Not Disturb, Bedtime/Rest and alarms).

## What WatchSync collects

**Nothing.** WatchSync has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting. It does not collect, store remotely, sell or share any personal data.

## What stays on your devices

To work, WatchSync reads and keeps, only on your phone and your watch:

- which mode is on (Do Not Disturb, Rest/Bedtime) and the rules of the phone's Rest mode;
- whether an alarm is ringing, its time and label, and the time of the next alarm;
- the name of the paired device and whether it is reachable;
- the watch's Bluetooth address, used only so Android can show the right watch in the
  "manage this watch" confirmation;
- the app's settings (for example the language).

This information travels only between your phone and your watch, through Wear OS's own data
layer (Google Play services). WatchSync sends nothing anywhere else.

## Notifications

WatchSync uses notification access only to see which mode is on and whether an alarm is ringing.
It does not read, store or forward the content of your notifications.

## Permissions

Each permission is explained in the app (the ⓘ next to every setup step): notifications and
full-screen alarms to ring when the other device rings; notification and Do Not Disturb access
to read and change the modes; local network and nearby devices only during the one-time watch
setup, to reach the watch's wireless debugging and find the paired watch.

## Backups

Android's backup may save WatchSync's settings with your other app data. The live state of the
devices is not backed up.

## Children

WatchSync is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the project repository:
https://github.com/SanniniciStyle/WatchSync — questions can be opened as issues there.
